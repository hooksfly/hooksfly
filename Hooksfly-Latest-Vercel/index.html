Privacy Policy

Updated 13 October 2026

HooksFly is an independent digital marketing and creative services brand operated by Sumit Poddar. It is not a registered company. This policy explains how information shared through this website is handled.

  1. Information you share

    Our enquiry form collects your name, phone number, email address, monthly creative requirement and selected services. It also records your consent, the submission time and a reference ID.

  2. How we use it

    We use these details to respond to your enquiry, discuss your requirements and coordinate requested services. Submitting an enquiry does not create a paid order.

  3. Storage and service providers

    Enquiries are sent through Google Apps Script to a Google Sheet used by HooksFly. Google and our website hosting provider process technical information needed to operate these services. Anyone with access granted to the Sheet can view the stored enquiries.

  4. Your choices

    You can ask to review, correct or delete information you submitted, or ask us to stop contacting you, by emailing hooksfly@gmail.com. Include enough information for us to identify your enquiry; we may need to verify the request.

  5. Information to leave out

    Please do not submit passwords, payment-card details, government identification numbers or other sensitive information in an enquiry.

  6. External links

    Community links open WhatsApp. Your use of WhatsApp and other external services is governed by their own privacy policies.

  7. Security and updates

    No online transmission or storage method is completely secure. This policy may be updated when our website or data-handling practices change; the latest version will appear here.
